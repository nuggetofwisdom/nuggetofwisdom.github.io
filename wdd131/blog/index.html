Septimus Heap Book One: Magyk

by Angie Sage

If you enjoy stories about seventh sons of seventh sons and magyk, this is the book for you. On the night Septimus Heap is born, he is whisked away by a midwife and declared dead — the same night his father finds a mysterious baby girl in the snow. Ten years later, a young apprentice, a reluctant princess, and a powerful ExtraOrdinary Wizard are pulled into an adventure full of charms, spells, and secrets.
